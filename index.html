5 weekend escapes from Bengaluru this October

The Deccan Wanderer · updated this month · 6 min read

1. Goa

Shacks reopen through October and prices stay below the December peak. North Goa gets busy on long weekends; the south (Agonda, Palolem) stays calm. 2. Kochi

Fort Kochi's heritage walks and the backwaters around Alappuzha are at their greenest just after the rains. Pair it with a night on a houseboat.

3. Hyderabad

Cooler evenings make this the month for the old-city food trail and the Golconda light-and-sound show.

4. Mangaluru & Udupi

Temple towns, quiet beaches and the best ghee roast you'll eat. Short flight, small airport, easy weekend.

5. Pondicherry (via Chennai)

Lovely, but check the weather: the north-east monsoon can bring heavy rain to the Tamil Nadu coast from mid-October.
